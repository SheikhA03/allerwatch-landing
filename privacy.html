Privacy Policy — AllerWatch

Last updated: November 19, 2026

AllerWatch ("we", "us", "the app") respects your privacy. This document explains what data the app collects, why, and your rights regarding it.

AllerWatch helps you log meals and food reactions so you can spot patterns in what triggers your allergies. Because of this, some of the data the app handles is health-adjacent — specifically, the food reactions and symptoms you choose to log. We treat this data with particular care: we do not sell it, we do not share it with anyone beyond the service providers listed below (each of which processes it only on our behalf, to run the app), and you can request its deletion at any time.

2. How we use your data

  • To create, secure, and let you sign in to your account.
  • To store your meal and reaction history so the app can function as a food/allergy log.
  • To detect patterns in your own history and surface likely trigger foods and cooking methods.
  • To resolve a dish name into likely ingredients when you log a meal.
  • To manage your subscription and unlock premium features.
  • To send you the daily reaction-reminder notification, if enabled.
  • To understand app usage and fix bugs, via analytics and crash reporting.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. The third-party services listed above process data on our behalf, strictly to provide the functionality described:

  • Supabase (Supabase Inc., supabase.com/privacy) — authentication and database hosting for your account, meals, allergies, and reactions. Data is hosted in the ap-northeast-1 (Tokyo) region.
  • Google Sign-In / Android Speech Recognizer (Google LLC, policies.google.com/privacy) — sign-in, and on-device/system speech-to-text for voice meal logging.
  • OpenRouter (openrouter.ai/privacy), which routes the dish-name text to Google Gemini for ingredient resolution — only the dish name itself is sent (e.g. "chicken karahi"), never your name, email, or health/reaction data.
  • RevenueCat (revenuecat.com/privacy) — subscription and payment management.
  • PostHog (PostHog Inc., posthog.com/privacy) — product analytics.
  • Firebase (Google LLC, firebase.google.com/support/privacy) — analytics, crash reporting (Crashlytics), and push notifications (Cloud Messaging).

None of these providers are permitted to use your data for their own purposes beyond providing the service to us.

4. Your rights

You can:

  • Delete your account and all associated data at any time by emailing attaf.sheikh03@gmail.com.
  • Opt out of analytics from within the app's settings.
  • Request a copy of your data by emailing the same address.

Data is fully purged within 30 days of a deletion request.

5. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) between the app and every service listed above. Any secrets stored locally on your device are encrypted at rest using Android Keystore-backed AES-GCM encryption.

6. Children

AllerWatch is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

7. International transfers / GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to access, correct, delete, or export your data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside the EEA (including Japan, where our database is hosted, and the United States, where some of our service providers operate) under appropriate safeguards. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at attaf.sheikh03@gmail.com.

8. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change.
