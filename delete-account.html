Delete Your Account — AllerWatch

Last updated: August 10, 2026

You can request deletion of your AllerWatch account and all associated data at any time. This applies whether you signed in with Google or any other method AllerWatch supports.

How to request deletion

Send an email to attaf.sheikh03@gmail.com from the email address associated with your AllerWatch account, with the subject line "Delete my account." Include your account email address in the body if it differs from the sending address.

What gets deleted

Once your request is confirmed, we permanently delete:

  • Your account and sign-in information (name, email, user ID)
  • Your known allergies
  • All logged meals, ingredients, and cooking methods
  • All logged reactions, symptoms, severity, and notes
  • Your subscription and purchase history records

Retention period

Deletion is completed within 30 days of a confirmed request. Some minimal billing records may be retained longer only where required by law (for example, tax or fraud-prevention obligations tied to processed payments), and are not used for any other purpose.

Questions

Contact attaf.sheikh03@gmail.com with any questions about this process.
